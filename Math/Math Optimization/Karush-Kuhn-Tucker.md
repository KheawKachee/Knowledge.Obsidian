## Summary
> [!abstract] 
> **Kuhn–Tucker conditions**, are [first derivative tests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First-order_condition "First-order condition") (sometimes called first-order [necessary conditions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Necessary_and_sufficient_conditions "Necessary and sufficient conditions")) for a solution in [nonlinear programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_programming "Nonlinear programming") to be [optimal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimization_\(mathematics\) "Optimization (mathematics)"), provided that some [regularity conditions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karush%E2%80%93Kuhn%E2%80%93Tucker_conditions#Regularity_conditions_\(or_constraint_qualifications\)) are satisfied.


### Conditions

- Stationarity : $\nabla_x \mathcal{L}(x, \lambda) = 0$

- Primal feasibility: $g_i(x) \le 0$

- Dual feasibility: $\lambda_i \ge 0$

- Complementary slackness : $\lambda_i g_i(x) = 0$
