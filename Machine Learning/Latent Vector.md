### Summary

> [!abstract] 
> A latent vector is a compressed, lower-dimensional numerical representation of high-dimensional data, capturing its essential underlying features.


### Example
- 512 latent vectors of 1M pixels picture of dog.

### vs feature

[[Feature]] is something that human can interpret , unlike latent vectors which is numerical complex.
