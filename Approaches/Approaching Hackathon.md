### **Summary**

> [!abstract]
> Only strategy for hackathons and data science competitions, focusing on the alignment between technical execution and narrative delivery. Success is defined by the intersection of meeting host expectations, maintaining technical rigor, and communicating complex solutions through a cohesive, story-driven pitch.

### **First Thing to Do**

* **Analyze Competition Rubrics:** Identify the specific weighting for different criteria
* **Define Deliverables:** Clarify exactly what must be submitted 
* **Research Host & Judges:** Study their industry background and previous projects to **tailor the solution's "Pain Point"** to their specific interests or technical values.

### **Pitch Deck**

* **Narrative Flow:** Ensure a clear, data-driven story where each slide builds on the last, connecting the Problem → Methodology → Results → Impact.
* **Technical Integrity:** Balance high-level value propositions with technical depth. Be prepared to defend your model architecture, data cleaning process, and validation strategy 
* **Visual Clarity:** Use clean charts and diagrams to simplify complex architectures or causal relationships.

### **At Stage**

* **The "Elevator" Hook:** Be able to **wrap the entire project topic in one sentence** that captures both the *what* and the *why*.
* **Confidence in Constraints:** Anticipate technical questions regarding model limitations, sustainability, or edge cases.
* **Presence:** Focus on clarity and pacing; ensure the technical solution feels like a tool designed for real-world application, not just a theoretical exercise.
